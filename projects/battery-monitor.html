Project 06 · Embedded systems · 2024

Car Battery Monitor

An embedded system that reads a car battery’s voltage in real time and shows its health on an LCD — before a flat battery surprises you.

Source code

Overview

A PIC16F877A microcontroller measures the battery through a voltage divider, converts the reading with its 10-bit ADC and prints both the voltage and a health status on a 16 × 2 LCD. I designed the divider circuit and wrote the firmware in embedded C.

The photo is the working prototype on the bench, reading 13.3 V — Status: OK.

How it works

  1. ScaleA 1 : 4 voltage divider brings the battery voltage into the ADC’s 0–5 V range on pin RA0/AN0.
  2. MeasureThe 10-bit ADC samples the input against VDD/VSS and the firmware multiplies the result back by four.
  3. ClassifyThe reading is mapped to one of six health states (table below).
  4. DisplayVoltage on the first LCD row, status on the second — refreshed every second and redrawn only when the value moves by 0.1 V or more.

Health states

Battery voltageLCD status
above 14.8 VHIGH
14.4 – 14.8 VCHARGING
13.8 – 14.4 VGOOD
12.6 – 13.8 VOK
11.5 – 12.6 VLOW
below 11.5 VCRITICAL
