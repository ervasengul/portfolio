06 View project
Embedded systems PIC16F877A · C · 2024

Car Battery Monitor

An embedded system on a PIC16F877A that reads car-battery voltage in real time and shows its health — from CRITICAL to CHARGING — on an LCD. I designed the voltage-divider circuit that brings the battery into the ADC's 0–5 V range and wrote the firmware in embedded C.
