01 · Website · brand · content platform

It Girl City Guides

itgirlcityguide.com · launching soon
itgirlcityguide.com

A digital travel-guide brand: European cities mapped neighbourhood by neighbourhood, written for a specific three-to-five-day trip instead of forty open tabs. I designed the brand and the site — a Bodoni wordmark, hand-drawn tile illustrations for every city, a guide layout with a free “read it, test it” section before the paid download, and an email list for new city drops.

Built as a static site with Astro and Tailwind, deployed from GitHub Actions. Fifteen cities in the catalogue, Copenhagen written first.

02 · Website · fashion brand

Bury Bella

Visit burybella.com
burybella.com

A one-screen launch page for Bury Bella, a streetwear label. The whole page is a single night-shot photograph of the piece, a “SHOP NOW” button and a slow “DROP LIVE” ticker running down both edges — nothing else to read, one thing to do. The button hands off to the label’s shop on Shopier. On phones the photo swaps for a portrait shot and the ticker moves to the top and bottom.

Hand-written HTML and CSS, no framework, hosted on GitHub Pages with the brand’s own domain.

04 · Mobile app · UI design

Yedik: The App

Engineering details

A social restaurant-rating app for Istanbul, designed around one idea: your feed is only the people you follow. I designed every screen — a warm burgundy and cream palette, a serif wordmark, photo-led cards with a score out of ten, and a short onboarding that explains the app in three steps.

Then I built it: the React Native app, the Go API and the database behind it. The engineering side is on the project page.
